# Systematic Trading Research

A research project investigating intraday futures strategies through historical backtesting, out-of-sample validation, and risk-constrained simulation.

## Current Focus

- Intraday NQ futures strategies
- Development vs. out-of-sample testing
- Transaction-cost modelling
- Drawdown and risk analysis
- Rule-constrained trading simulations
- Strategy robustness

## Research Approach

The objective is not to maximize historical backtest performance, but to test whether strategy behaviour is robust and generalizes beyond development data.

Negative out-of-sample results are retained and analysed rather than used for post-hoc optimisation.
